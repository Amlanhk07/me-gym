MacroDailyPurpose

Best Proteins

  • Eggs (5-6 daily)
  • Hilsa, Rohu, Catla
  • Chicken breast
  • Daal + rice combo
  • Doi / Greek yogurt

Avoid

  • Cola, juice, sugary drinks
  • Fried snacks, white bread
  • Daily mishti/sweets
  • Heavy rice at dinner
  • Late-night eating

Timing

  • Pre: banana + black coffee
  • Post: eggs + rice within 1h
  • Don't train fully fasted

Strength Tips

  • Compound lifts first
  • 60-90s rest between sets

Belly Fat

  • More: green tea, eggs, fish
  • Avoid liquid calories
  • Protein with every meal
  • Time carbs around workouts

Recovery

  • Sleep 7-8h non-negotiable
  • 70% T made during sleep
  • Wed + Sun are rest days
  • 20 min morning sun = Vit D
