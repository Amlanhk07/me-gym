02 Cardio Machine Guide
🏃

Treadmill

  • Speed 5.5–6.5 for fat burn zone
  • Incline 4–6 burns more without running fast
  • Speed 3 is too slow — barely any calorie burn
🚴

Cycling / Bike

  • Set resistance to medium-high
  • 30s sprints → 90s easy × 10 rounds = HIIT
  • Knee-friendly and great for testosterone
⚙️

Cross Trainer

  • Full-body, zero impact — excellent fat burn
  • Keep RPM above 60
  • Use arm handles actively, don't lean on them

04 Boosting Testosterone Naturally
🏋️

Train Heavy

Squats, deadlifts, bench press trigger the largest testosterone response. Heavy compound lifts beat cardio for hormone health.

😴

Sleep 7–8 Hours

70% of testosterone is produced during sleep. Even 1 week of 5–6h sleep drops T levels by 15%. Non-negotiable.

🥚

Eat Healthy Fats

Testosterone is synthesized from cholesterol. Eggs, fish, nuts, and olive oil are essential. Never go very low-fat.

☀️

Morning Sunlight

20–30 min of morning sun daily. Vitamin D deficiency is directly linked to low testosterone. Free and powerful.

🧘

Manage Stress

Cortisol directly suppresses testosterone. Rest days aren't optional — they're when your hormones recover and grow.

🥦

Zinc & Magnesium

Pumpkin seeds, red meat, spinach, almonds. These are directly involved in testosterone synthesis — deficiency is common in active men.

05 Apple Watch: Tracking Workouts

How to Track Every Session

1
Open the Workout app

Press Digital Crown → find green "Workout" icon. No phone needed.

2
For Cardio Machines

Treadmill → "Indoor Walk/Run" · Cycling → "Indoor Cycle" · Cross Trainer → "Elliptical"

3
For Weight Training

Select "Strength Training" — tracks heart rate, active calories, and time accurately.

4
Can't find it? Add it

Scroll to bottom of Workout app → "Add Workout" → pin your most-used ones to the top.

5
Best companion app: Strong

Download Strong (free, iOS) to log sets, reps, and weight. Pairs with Apple Health automatically.

📱

Recommended Apps

  • Strong — log weights & reps
  • MyFitnessPal — calorie tracking
  • Apple Health — see all data
🎯

Key Metrics to Watch

  • Active Calories: target 400–600/session
  • Heart Rate: 130–150 BPM fat burn zone
  • Resting HR: should drop over weeks
