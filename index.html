            02 Nutrition

            Daily Macro Targets

            Maintenance ~2,400 kcal. Target ~1,900 kcal/day for steady fat loss.

            MacroDailyWhy
            Protein140–160gPreserves muscle, keeps you full
            Carbs160–180gFuel for workouts. Rice, oats, veg
            Fats50–60gCritical for testosterone
            Water3–4 litresBangladesh heat — stay hydrated
            🍳

            Best Proteins

            • Eggs (5–6 daily)
            • Hilsa, Rohu, Catla fish
            • Chicken breast
            • Daal + rice combo
            • Doi / Greek yogurt
            🚫

            Avoid

            • Cola & sweetened drinks
            • Fried snacks
            • Daily mishti/sweets
            • Heavy rice at dinner
            • Late-night eating
            ⚡

            Pre / Post

            • Pre: banana + black coffee 30 min before
            • Post: eggs + rice within 1 hour
            • Don't train fully fasted
            03 Boost Testosterone
            🏋️

            Train Heavy

            Squats, deadlifts, bench press trigger the biggest T response. Compound lifts beat cardio for hormones.

            😴

            Sleep 7–8h

            70% of testosterone is made during sleep. Even one week at 5–6h drops T by 15%. Non-negotiable.

            ☀️

            Morning Sun

            20–30 min daily. Vitamin D deficiency directly linked to low T. Free and powerful in Bangladesh.

            🧘

            Manage Stress

            Cortisol suppresses testosterone. Rest days are when your hormones recover — don't skip them.
